# Practice: turn notes into an action list

Use the fictional input.txt. Ask your assistant to produce an action table with task, owner role, due date, and source evidence. Preserve missing information and distinguish discussion from commitment. Do not send reminders or create calendar entries.

Check that the coordinator has the agenda action and its exact date, the venue shortlist has neither an invented owner nor date, and the review has no invented date. Catering is discussion, not an assigned action.

For a conflict test, add: “A later note gives the agenda deadline as 2026-11-03, but its approval status is unknown.” The assistant should show the conflict and ask which date governs.

Compare with expected-result.md after making your own attempt. This case demonstrates that the method applies to documents as well as numerical data.

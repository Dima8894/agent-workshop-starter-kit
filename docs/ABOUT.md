# Dmitrii Titov

Dmitrii Titov is an AI practitioner, product builder, and corporate AI educator. He runs practical workshops for company teams and works on MCP connectors and frontend tools. His approach starts with a business task, the context needed to perform it, and a way to verify the result.

His work includes Slota, which connects to Codex through MCP and provides structured context and methods. Hybrid memory across company and personal workflows is a development direction, not a capability this starter kit implements.

Agent Workshop / AI Starter Kit is his English-language open-source contribution for beginners and teams learning to work with AI. It extends the original Alpes workshop starter with an adaptive interview, a reusable workspace architecture, and a path toward team adoption.

## Why I made this kit

I want people to leave with something they can use. Start with one task. Give the assistant the information it needs. Check the result yourself. Then try it again with a new input. That is where a useful working system begins.
